Controls

ActionTouch
MoveFloating thumbstick. Press anywhere in the lower-left of the screen and drag.
JumpThe ⌃ button.
AttackThe sword button. Costs stamina.
Shield / ParryThe shield button. Tap it, do not hold it. Costs stamina.
Power StrikeThe flame button. You start each stage with one, and power strike packs refill it. Most enemies cannot block it.
Ember DashThe streak button above the thumbstick. Unlocked by defeating Ashhorn (Stage 2) and available from Stage 3.
PauseThe II button, top-right. Resume, restart the stage, or return to the main menu.

Frequently asked questions

How do I parry?

Tap the shield a split second before an enemy's hit lands. The shield only stays up for a moment, then drops by itself, and it takes a moment before you can raise it again, so spamming does not work. A clean parry takes no damage, refunds your stamina and counter-hits the attacker. Hits from behind can never be blocked.

Why does Ember keep getting tired?

Attacking, raising the shield and dashing all spend stamina, and it refills slowly once you stop. When you run out of stamina, your swing or guard becomes a clumsy fumble that leaves you open. Back off, let the bar refill, then go again.

My sword passes right through the Shade Wraith.

Shade Wraiths are only solid in the light. Strike a crystal brazier with your sword to light it, or use your Power Strike, which lights every brazier at once. While a wraith stands in the light you can hurt it.

How does saving work? How do I continue?

The game saves a checkpoint each time you clear a stage. Choose Continue on the main menu to resume from your latest checkpoint, or New Game to start over. Once you have finished the game, a Stage Select option lets you replay any stage.

Your progress is stored on your device only. It is not tied to an account, so deleting the app normally erases it unless you restore the app from a device backup.

How do I reset my progress?

Main menu → Options → Reset Progress, then confirm.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

There are no in-app purchases. The game is free and supported by ads: an optional ad you can watch to revive after a defeat (offered once each time you launch the game), and a short ad after Stage 2. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the game simply carries on without one.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

In the game: main menu → Options → Personalised Ads (On / Off).

On your device: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then switch Knight of the Hollow on or off. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The “Revive (Ad)” button is missing.

The revive is offered once per app launch, only while the stage can still be won, and only when an ad has loaded. Check your internet connection. Closing the ad early does not grant the revive. Fully closing and reopening the game gives you a new offer.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 15.0 or later. The game plays in landscape orientation only.

The game feels too hard.

It is meant to be tough but fair: every enemy telegraphs its attacks. Watch the wind-up, parry on the swing, then punish. Keep a little stamina in reserve, save your Power Strike for a boss, and pick up the health packs that drop in the later waves.

Will there be more levels?

Yes. This is chapter one of Ember's story, and more levels are coming soon.

The game crashes, freezes or looks wrong.

Close the game fully and reopen it, and make sure you are on the latest version of Knight of the Hollow and iOS. If the problem continues, email us with your device model, iOS version and the stage where it happens.

How do I delete my data?

We do not run accounts or servers that hold your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Use Reset Progress in Options, or delete the app, to remove everything the game stores on your device. For data held by our advertising partners, see the “Your choices and rights” section of the Privacy Policy.
