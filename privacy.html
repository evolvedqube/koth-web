Knight of the Hollow

Privacy Policy

Support Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version. Knight of the Hollow has no accounts, no sign-in and no purchases. Your game progress and settings stay on your device. The game shows ads through third-party advertising partners, and those partners collect some device and ad-related data to deliver and measure ads. You choose whether the ads are personalised. To improve the game, we also measure how players progress through its stages, using a random ID that is not linked to you. Details are below.

Who we are

Knight of the Hollow (the “game”) is published by Evolved Qube Tech Inc (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses and the choices you have. If you have a question, contact us at evolvedqube@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only. It is not sent to us:

  • your progress (which stage checkpoint you have reached, and whether you have finished the game);
  • your music volume setting;
  • your answer to the “Allow personalised ads?” question.

You can erase your progress at any time with Options → Reset Progress, or by deleting the app.

Information we collect from you

We do not ask for, and the game does not collect, your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or any account details. We run no servers of our own that store information about you. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, and we use it only to reply and to improve the game.

Gameplay analytics

To see which stages players find hard, the game uses GameAnalytics, an analytics service that processes data on our behalf. It receives an event each time a stage is started, completed, failed or left, how long the attempt took, and how many attempts the stage needed. Each install is identified by a random ID that the game creates on your device; it is not linked to your name, email address or any account. Like any app SDK, GameAnalytics also receives basic device and app information (device model, iOS version, app version, language, country derived from your IP address, and a vendor identifier specific to apps from the same developer) and, only if you allowed tracking in the iOS prompt, your device’s advertising identifier. Events are stored on your device while you are offline and sent later. We use this data only to understand and improve the game; it is not used for advertising. It is kept according to the GameAnalytics privacy policy.

Advertising and our partners

The game is free and supported by ads. We show an optional rewarded ad (to revive after a defeat) and a short ad after Stage 2. To do this, the game includes the Unity LevelPlay advertising SDK, which connects to ad networks (currently ironSource and Unity Ads) and includes ironSource Ad Quality, which checks ads for quality and fraud. These partners act as independent controllers of the data they collect for advertising, under their own privacy policies.

When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below), and a vendor identifier (IDFV) that is specific to apps from the same developer;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location (such as country or city) that can be derived from it;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, app version and network type;
  • an identifier the partner assigns to you or your device;
  • how you use the game, such as app launches and taps;
  • ad interaction data: whether ads were requested, shown, clicked, completed or closed, and related performance and fraud-prevention signals;
  • information about purchases or purchase tendencies, which partners may use to choose ads (the game itself has no purchases).

They use this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, prevent fraud, pay the correct amounts, and, only if you allow it, personalise ads. We receive reports about ad performance and revenue from these partners, but not information that identifies you.

Read their policies for full details: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and the Unity Privacy Policy (covering Unity LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads).

Tracking and your choices

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

When you first open the game, iOS may ask whether the game may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you tap Allow, our advertising partners may use your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and to measure them, and may combine it with data from other sources. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you are not tracked across other companies’ apps and websites. Ads may still use non-tracking information such as your country, language or device type. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Personalised ads consent

The game asks once whether to allow personalised ads. Choosing No tells our advertising partners not to personalise ads and, for players in the United States, not to sell or share your personal information. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Options → Personalised Ads. The choice is passed to our advertising partners immediately.

Even with personalised ads off, you will still see ads. They are just not based on your interests.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a US state such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data held about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal data for targeted advertising. Sharing an advertising identifier with ad partners can count as “sharing” or “sale” under some of these laws. You can opt out at any time by setting Personalised Ads to Off in the game, which sends a “do not sell or share” signal to our advertising partners. Choosing Ask App Not to Track also stops your advertising identifier from being shared.

We do not keep your personal data ourselves, so there is generally nothing for us to access or delete. To make a request about data held by our advertising partners, or if you need help using the controls above, email evolvedqube@gmail.com or contact the partner through their privacy policy. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

How long data is kept

Data stored on your device stays until you reset your progress or delete the app. Data collected by advertising partners and by GameAnalytics is kept according to their own policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data for money and do not share information with anyone except as described in this policy: our advertising partners and GameAnalytics as above, and when the law requires it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Security

We keep what we hold to a minimum. Data on your device is protected by your device’s own security, and our partners are responsible for securing the data they collect.

International transfers

Our advertising partners may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. They are responsible for appropriate safeguards under their policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, for example by adding new features or partners, we will update this page and the date at the top. We recommend checking back from time to time.
